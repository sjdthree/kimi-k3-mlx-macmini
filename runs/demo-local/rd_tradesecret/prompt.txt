Board strategy context
HelioForge Systems develops high-density liquid-cooled AI compute modules. Management believes the company's most
valuable intellectual property may not be captured by its filed patents because several performance improvements emerged as
manufacturing and control-system know-how. The board wants an integrated R&D; / trade-secret / invention strategy before
the next financing.

1. Existing patent portfolio - simplified claims
      Family                 Core claimed concept

      HF-101                 Cold plate with parallel microchannels beneath accelerator package.

      HF-118                 Rack manifold with redundant pumps and pressure sensors.

      HF-133                 Controller that adjusts coolant flow as a function of measured package temperature.

2. Confidential R&D; notebook excerpts
Experiment 26-041: During rapid workload transitions, package hotspot temperature overshoots even when average coolant
flow is increased. Engineer L. Chen observed that the derivative of GPU power demand predicts the hotspot 180-260 ms
before the package temperature sensor responds.
Experiment 26-052: A controller using workload power derivative plus inlet-fluid compressibility estimate reduced
99.9th-percentile hotspot overshoot by 37% relative to temperature-feedback control. The effect disappeared when the
accumulator pre-charge was changed by more than 12%.
Experiment 26-061: Manufacturing found that intentionally varying microchannel width across the cold plate by +/-6%
counteracted package-level heat-flux asymmetry. The CAD variation is generated from each package's measured
post-assembly thermal map, not from a fixed geometry.

3. Manufacturing process notes
The adaptive channel-width map is generated after package attach. A proprietary script converts thermal-map coordinates into
laser-machining offsets. Operators see only a job identifier; the transformation coefficients are stored in an access-controlled
build server. Yield improved from 91.2% to 97.8% after introduction.
A supplier receives final machining coordinates but not the thermal map, algorithm, or coefficients. The supplier contract
prohibits reverse engineering and reuse of job files. Coordinates are unique to each unit and retained by the supplier for 30
days.

5. Commercial and reverse-engineering observations
Customers can measure external coolant pressure, inlet/outlet temperature, and application performance. They cannot directly
inspect firmware because secure boot and signed updates are enabled. Destructive teardown reveals the physical channel
geometry, although inferring the per-unit generation algorithm would require comparing multiple units with their pre-machining
thermal maps, which customers do not possess.
A competitor recently advertised 'predictive liquid cooling using workload telemetry' but has not disclosed its algorithm. No
known competitor advertises per-package adaptive cold-plate geometry.

6. Internal invention disclosure status
No invention disclosure has been filed for the feed-forward controller, compressibility estimator, accumulator interaction, or
per-unit geometry-generation method. Engineering has treated all four as implementation details. Sales presentations disclose
that HelioForge achieves 'predictive cooling' and 'factory-calibrated cold plates' but do not disclose the mechanisms.
