Board incident-review context
Aster Vale Health develops an adaptive sepsis deterioration model deployed at Northbridge Medical Center. A serious adverse
event occurred after release 4.8. The model executed the configured policy as designed; the open question is whether the
failure originated in the model, software implementation, clinical policy, deployment controls, or human use.

1. Cleared / approved operating envelope
      Item                             Documented requirement

      Intended use                     Risk stratification support for adult inpatients; not a stand-alone diagnostic.

      Alert policy                     High-risk alert when calibrated 6-hour deterioration probability >=0.35.

      Adaptive update                  Monthly recalibration of intercept and calibration slope permitted; feature set and clinical action policy locked.

      Override                         Hospital may suppress alerts only through validated unit-level configuration with clinical governance approval.

2. Hospital clinical policy excerpt
Northbridge policy NMC-SEP-17, revision 2024-11, instructs nursing staff to defer escalation when an alert occurs within four
hours after initiation of a documented fluid-resuscitation protocol unless specified instability criteria are present. In March 2026,
the hospital's sepsis committee revised its fluid protocol and shortened reassessment to two hours, but NMC-SEP-17 was not
updated.

      Monthly calibration refresh                 Implemented; no feature or threshold changes.

      Alert suppression rule                      Reads hospital-configured suppression interval from policy service.

      Fallback if policy service unavailable      Uses last validated configuration.
