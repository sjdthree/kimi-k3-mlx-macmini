Executive context
Orion Ridge Semiconductor operates a 300 mm pilot line for a proprietary low-k interconnect module. Lot OR-26-091 showed
a sudden increase in post-etch bridge defects. Yield engineering initially suspected pattern density because the failures
appeared more often in outer die. The purpose of this package is to determine the most likely physical mechanism and the
minimum discriminating experiments.

4. Engineering notes and distractors
The lithography team reported no scanner overlay excursion. Incoming low-k material passed viscosity and solids
specifications. CMP removal-rate maps were within historical control. Ambient humidity was 6% above the monthly mean, but
other lots exposed to the same ambient window did not show the defect. A new photoresist lot was introduced two days earlier
and was used on both affected and unaffected chamber splits.
A facilities work order records a brief chilled-water pressure fluctuation on Sep 15 at 09:12. Tool telemetry shows no
corresponding chuck-temperature excursion. A chamber engineer informally noted that the replacement edge seal 'felt slightly
proud' during installation but the standard PM checklist contains no quantitative seating-height measurement.
